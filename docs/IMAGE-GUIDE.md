# TribeInn Website Image Guide — V2

## Brand hierarchy
**TribeInn** is the parent stay brand. **The Beginning** is its first Goa stay.
Rio De Goa is the community/development in which The Beginning is located; it is not a separate TribeInn property page.

## Non-negotiable truthfulness rules
1. Use real TribeInn / Rio De Goa photography. Do not invent rooms, views or amenities with AI.
2. Never imply that Goa beach photography is a view from the apartment.
3. Do not advertise or show the rooftop infinity pool; it is not available to TribeInn guests.
4. Main pool, gym and terrace/viewing area may be presented as guest-accessible amenities based on owner confirmation.
5. Do not describe an amenity as guest-accessible merely because a photograph exists. The indoor common/lounge image is supporting material until access is confirmed.
6. Preserve room reality. Crop, straighten, resize and make restrained photographic corrections only.

Use washing-machine and utility-balcony photographs to support the long-stay proposition. These are practical proof points, not hero imagery.

### Bathroom / practical
Use selectively in galleries, amenities and practical information.

## Goa Around Us
`assets/images/goa-around-us/goa-beach-01.webp` is destination imagery only.
Do not build the full Goa Around Us section yet; dedicated local content will be supplied separately.

## Astra / Codex implementation rules
- Prefer large, confident photographs over dense gallery grids.
- Keep apartment photography and Rio De Goa community photography visually distinguishable.
- TribeInn should feel colourful, handmade, personal and Goan — not generic beige luxury.
- Long-stay usefulness should emerge naturally through work space, full kitchen, storage and laundry.
- Do not substitute stock or AI-generated property photography.
